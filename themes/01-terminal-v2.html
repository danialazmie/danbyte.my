About

I'm a data scientist working across the full data stack — from building data platforms, to training ML models, to shipping GenAI applications in production. My focus is bridging the gap between advanced AI/ML systems and real business needs: making models useful, not just accurate.

Over the past ~4.5 years I've worked across fintech, media, consulting and edtech — migrating pipelines, standing up MLOps on the cloud, building recommendation engines, and lately, building RAG chatbots and AI agents that talk to company data through MCP.

Now

Right now I'm most interested in AI agents that work on top of real company data, and in the unglamorous data plumbing that makes them trustworthy. I like owning the whole path from raw events to a decision someone actually makes, and I'm looking for teams where that full stack, warehouse to agent, is the job.
